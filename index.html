About me

I teach computing in the Faculty of Engineering & Technology at Muscat University and hold a doctorate in information technology. My research spans educational technology, data analytics, and cybersecurity.

I designed and evaluated a discussion platform for collaborative learning and have contributed to funded studies on digital transformation, AI in higher education, and technology adoption in Oman.

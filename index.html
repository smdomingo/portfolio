About me

I am an Assistant Professor in the Faculty of Engineering & Technology at Muscat University and hold a doctorate in information technology. My research spans educational technology, data analytics, and cybersecurity.

My research includes designing and evaluating a discussion platform for collaborative learning and contributing to funded projects on digital transformation and AI in higher education.

About me

I have more than 20 years of experience teaching information technology and computer science in higher education in Oman and the Philippines. My academic work includes curriculum development, assessment design, dissertation supervision, and research mentoring.

I earned a Doctor in Information Technology and a Master of Science in Computer Science from AMA University. My research spans educational technology, data analytics, cybersecurity, and the adoption of digital technologies.

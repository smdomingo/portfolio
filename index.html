Teaching philosophy

I teach computing through practice. I ask students to solve realistic problems, explain their choices, and use feedback to improve their work.

In cybersecurity, I want students to ask what they are protecting, who could be affected, and how they would justify their decisions. I set clear expectations and break complex tasks into stages so students can build confidence.

When I supervise dissertations, I help students shape their own questions, test their ideas carefully, and explain what the evidence supports.

About

I am a dedicated Assistant Professor, researcher, reviewer, curriculum developer, dissertation supervisor, and faculty mentor with over 24 years of teaching experience in higher education. My academic work is strongly connected by technology, particularly in the areas of software engineering, business analytics, digital marketing, artificial intelligence, educational technology, and applied computing.

Throughout my career, I have developed expertise in module leadership, instructional planning, assessment design, curriculum enhancement, student mentoring, academic advising, and quality assurance. I have supervised many dissertation and final-year projects, guiding students in research methodology, system development, testing, evaluation, and academic presentation. I also take pride in mentoring students through undergraduate research grants, conference presentations, and publication opportunities.

As an active researcher and reviewer, I have published in peer-reviewed journals and contributed to funded research initiatives. I have successfully participated in research grants and mentored undergraduate research projects under the Block Funding Programme of the Ministry of Higher Education, Research and Innovation (MoHERI). These experiences have strengthened my commitment to applied research, innovation, and student-centred academic development.

Beyond teaching and research, I am actively involved in curriculum development, academic committees, conference organization, accreditation-related activities, and institutional quality assurance. My experience with PACUCUA and OAAA-related quality standards has shaped my commitment to continuous improvement, academic excellence, and meaningful contribution to higher education in Oman and international academic contexts.
